Thread RF Channel Migration FAQ

Why migrate to Channel 25 or 26? Standard 20 MHz Wi-Fi access points on channels 1, 6, and 11 flood the 2.4 GHz spectrum with high transmit power (100–1000 mW), while Thread devices transmit at 1–10 mW. Channel 25 sits in the guard band above Wi-Fi Channel 11, and Channel 26 sits completely outside standard 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi channels, drastically reducing packet collisions and battery drain on sleepy end devices.

What happens to battery-powered sensors (Sleepy End Devices)? In Thread 1.3/1.4, when a Pending Dataset with a Delay Timer is broadcast, all parent routers maintain the new channel parameters. When the timer expires, parent routers and end devices switch together. If an end device was asleep during the broadcast, its parent informs it on its next poll.

What if I have Google Nest or Apple Border Routers? If you have multiple border routers sharing the same Extended PAN ID, the Thread Leader coordinates the operational dataset. Changing the channel on the ZBT-1 updates the dataset for the shared mesh fabric.
